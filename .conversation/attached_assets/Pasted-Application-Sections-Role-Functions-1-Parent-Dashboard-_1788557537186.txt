Application Sections & Role Functions
1. Parent Dashboard

My Children Profiles: Displays child's full name (read-only), current belt, activeness status, membership type, and payment status.

Coaches Feedback & Records: Inbox for receiving student performance updates, behavioral feedback, and attendance records from coaches.

(Includes Shared Modules: Financial System, Club News, Settings, About Us, Our Locations)

2. Student Dashboard

Profile View: Displays full name (read-only), current belt, activeness status, and membership payment status.

Classes Calendar: Interactive calendar showing class start and end times. Built around two primary schedules: (Sunday-Tuesday-Thursday) and (Saturday-Monday-Wednesday).

Coaches Feedback: Inbox mirroring the parent view, providing performance and tournament feedback to track weaknesses and improvements.

My Belt: Visual tracker showing current belt, historical belt progression, countdown/time remaining until the next belt test, and the associated testing cost.

(Includes Shared Modules: Financial System, Club News, Settings, About Us, Our Locations)

3. Coach Dashboard & Admin Tools

Work Schedule: Weekly timetable of assigned classes. Includes a "Time Off Request" feature allowing coaches to message peers for shift coverage, or agree on a mass day-off that pushes an alert to all affected students and parents.

My Students: A dedicated roster interface to track assigned students' progress, monitor their payment dues, and access parent contact info.

Financial Tracking: A dashboard to monitor roster subscriptions, specifically designed to push 1-week notice alerts for membership plan renewals.

Feedback Office: Text editor interface to write and send daily/weekly feedback (strengths, weaknesses, behavior) to students and parents. Also includes an interface to input physical paper attendance records into the digital system.

4. Shared Modules (Parents & Students)

Club News: Feed for announcements: tournaments, black belt testing dates, membership offers, vacations, and achievements by players and coaches.

Financial System: A ledger tracking all payments (memberships, equipment, club apparel) with exact dates and times. Includes online payment gateway UI for clearing dues and purchasing products.

Our Locations: Google Maps and Apple Maps deep-links for two branches: Tabarbour and North Marka.

About Us: Club history, leadership profile highlighting Grand Master Mahmoud Sqoor, and a showcase of club heroes who made the Jordan national team.

Settings: Standard app preferences.

CRITICAL LOGIC RULE: Users cannot edit their own names. Name changes are disabled in the UI and must be requested verbally for a coach to update via their admin privileges (restricted to spelling corrections).
